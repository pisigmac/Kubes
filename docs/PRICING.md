# Commercial Pricing & Packaging

## Plan Tiers

### 1. Developer (Free & Open Source)
- **Target**: Individual builders, researchers, and local developers.
- **Price**: $0 / month.
- **Includes**: Local execution, up to 5 custom Cubes, basic cron scheduling, local SQLite storage.

### 2. Pro / Team ($49 / month)
- **Target**: High-growth startups and small engineering teams.
- **Price**: $49 / month per organization.
- **Includes**: Up to 25 custom Cubes, 50 scheduled monitors, webhook digest broadcasts, multi-provider AI gateway.

### 3. Enterprise ($299 / month)
- **Target**: Security-conscious enterprises requiring strict data governance.
- **Price**: $299 / month (custom volume discounts available).
- **Includes**: Unlimited Cubes & schedules, full DeskID IAM / JWKS multi-tenant isolation, dedicated Chromium process sandboxing, 24/7 SLA.
